# Code of Conduct

ReplyDNA helps creators answer people kindly. We hold ourselves to the same standard.

**Be kind and constructive.** Critique ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and help newcomers.

**Not okay:** harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, publishing others' private
information, or sharing real viewers' comments or data without consent.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs, and may block contributors who break these rules.
Report problems privately to **hello@alcubix.com**; reports are handled confidentially.
