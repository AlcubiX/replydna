# Your offer: confirmed facts only
> Your course, membership, product, app, coaching or merch. Claude copies this to `config/offer.md` during setup
> and fills it from your website, then **you confirm every line**.
> ReplyDNA only uses lines marked ✅. Anything missing (discounts, next batch, refunds) gets
> "Let me confirm and get back to you" + a flag in your inbox. It never guesses a price.

| Fact | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Name | e.g. "The 30-Day Video Editing Bootcamp" | ⏳ confirm |
| One-line promise (no income claims) | e.g. "Edit like a pro in Premiere, step by step" | ⏳ confirm |
| Price | e.g. $99 one-time | ⏳ confirm |
| Format | e.g. 30 recorded lessons + weekly live Q&A | ⏳ confirm |
| Refund policy | e.g. 14-day refund | ⏳ confirm |
| Link | https://example.com/course | ⏳ confirm |
| Support contact for buyers | e.g. support@example.com | ⏳ confirm |
| Free resource to offer instead | e.g. free cheat-sheet link | ⏳ confirm |

No offer? Leave this file as it is. ReplyDNA will simply never mention one.
